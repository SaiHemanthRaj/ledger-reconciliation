# Provenance and expected anomalies
All records are fictional, generated for this project with random.Random(43). Data license: CC0 1.0.
1,000 orders, eight duplicate ledger records, five duplicate settlement records, ten amount mismatches,
ten orders paid in the wrong currency, ten overdue missing payments, ten pending payments, and ten orphan settlements.
Ten orders settle in two installments. There are 1,008 ledger rows and 1,005 settlement rows.
The demo cutoff is 2026-09-30; pending means order age is at most two days (inclusive).
There are no refunds, chargebacks, FX conversions, or bank fees in the model.
